# Surface Brief — Memoir Home (`src/app/page.tsx`)

- **Scope & Mode:** Single-page interactive photo memoir (`experience` / `read`)
- **Audience & Job:** College writing evaluation + personal keepsake for ten friends; needs emotional atmosphere, clear narrative pacing, and easy replacement of text/media placeholders.
- **Proof & Constraints:** Centralized `src/data/memoir.ts`, placeholders in `public/images/`, `public/videos/`, `public/audio/`, no fabricated memories, static export for GitHub Pages.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A tactile, multi-edition digital photo monograph of one ordinary week in Lake Tahoe before college, refusing generic SaaS card grids in favor of three switchable editorial print/cinema editions (Sun-Bleached Editorial, Cinematic Darkroom, Alpine Field Notes) driven by one narrative data source.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper cream (`#FAF8F3`), darkroom charcoal (`#141412`), and alpine stone (`#F4F5F0`) grounds; high-contrast literary serif display paired with humanist sans body and tabular monospace catalog indices; hairline rules, contact-sheet figure frames, and unobtrusive field-recording audio strips.
STORY: The reader enters the summer of 2026 in Lake Tahoe, moves through Familiarity (the surprise birthday), The Present (white water rafting), and Perspective (the ridge hike), and closes on the quiet realization that permanence was an illusion.
