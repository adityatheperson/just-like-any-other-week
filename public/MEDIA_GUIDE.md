# Media Asset Replacement Guide

Place your personal trip photographs, videos, and audio recordings into these three folders. All filenames match the paths configured in `src/data/memoir.ts`.

## `public/images/`
- `hero-lake-tahoe.jpg` — Hero opening full-screen photograph of Lake Tahoe
- `ch1-birthday-opening.jpg` — Chapter 1 opening photograph (surprise birthday setup / cabin evening)
- `ch1-gallery-1.jpg` — Chapter 1 gallery photo 1 (wide or portrait)
- `ch1-gallery-2.jpg` — Chapter 1 gallery photo 2 (candid)
- `ch1-gallery-3.jpg` — Chapter 1 gallery photo 3 (birthday table / detail)
- `ch1-gallery-4.jpg` — Chapter 1 gallery photo 4 (group candid)
- `ch2-rafting-opening.jpg` — Chapter 2 full-width dramatic white water rafting photograph
- `ch2-carousel-1.jpg` — Chapter 2 horizontal gallery photo 1
- `ch2-carousel-2.jpg` — Chapter 2 horizontal gallery photo 2
- `ch2-carousel-3.jpg` — Chapter 2 horizontal gallery photo 3
- `ch2-carousel-4.jpg` — Chapter 2 horizontal gallery photo 4
- `ch3-hike-opening.jpg` — Chapter 3 full-width landscape photograph from the Tahoe hike
- `ch3-hike-2.jpg` — Chapter 3 trail / overlook photograph
- `ch3-hike-3.jpg` — Chapter 3 quiet landscape / summit photograph
- `conclusion-group.jpg` — Conclusion large group photograph of all eleven friends

## `public/videos/`
- `hero-loop.mp4` — Optional muted background loop for the hero section
- `ch1-birthday.mp4` — Optional embedded video of the surprise birthday party
- `ch2-rafting.mp4` — Optional inline video clip from white water rafting
- `ch3-hike.mp4` — Optional quiet footage from the Tahoe hike

## `public/audio/`
- `ch2-river-ambient.mp3` — Optional ambient river / rafting audio clip for Chapter 2
- `ch3-trail-wind.mp3` — Optional natural wind / footsteps audio clip for Chapter 3

> **Note:** Until you drop real files into these folders, the website automatically renders clean, clearly labeled editorial placeholders so no broken images or fabricated photos ever appear.
